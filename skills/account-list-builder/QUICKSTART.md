# Build the Right Account List

Bring your company URL or Company Brain, an account CSV or bounded company source, and your inclusion/exclusion rules.

Install the individual skill ZIP with your AI tool's supported flow. Without an installer, attach `SKILL.md` and its references together.

> Use Account List Builder for {offering and campaign goal}. Our ICP is {qualifiers}; exclude {disqualifiers}. Use {attached candidate list or approved discovery scope}. Preserve original rows, apply relationship holds, and return a ranked company list with evidence, confidence, exceptions, and stable account IDs. Show the criteria and ask for my review. Do not enrich people, write to CRM, or launch outreach.

Review the list before giving it to Campaign Segmentation. If you do not have a list, define the source and discovery boundary first. A fit score is not buying intent or permission to contact.

Save the returned files in your approved workspace. If storage is not connected, download them or copy the named content blocks manually.

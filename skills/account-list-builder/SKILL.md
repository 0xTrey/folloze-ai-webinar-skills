# Account List Builder

Choose which companies belong in a campaign and explain why. This creates the audience; Campaign Segmentation groups the reviewed audience afterward.

Read [context-and-evidence.md](references/context-and-evidence.md) and [tool-map-use.md](references/tool-map-use.md). Use [output-template.md](references/output-template.md) for the handoff. Reuse the participant's Company Brain and supplied account IDs.

## 1. Set the targeting decision

Start with the offering, campaign goal, ICP qualifiers, disqualifiers, geography, size boundaries, and relationship rules. Accept an approved seller summary when a Company Brain is unavailable. Separate confirmed rules from proposed criteria.

Use the supplied list first. If there is no list, ask for a bounded company source or discovery scope. Do not expand a narrow list into unrestricted prospecting. Return a useful ICP draft while missing inputs are collected.

## 2. Reconcile the candidates

Keep each original row and source reference. Normalize company names and domains for comparison. Flag duplicates and missing identities; preserve parent/subsidiary distinctions and conflicting domains for review.

Apply supplied suppressions and explicit exclusions before scoring. Distinguish current customers, former customers, open opportunities, and unknown relationships using available dated records. Do not assume a universal exclusion rule or current status from an old association.

## 3. Gather company evidence

Use approved files and available primary company sources to check the criteria that change inclusion. Record exact source locations, dates, and limits. A disconnected CRM or an unobserved technology remains Missing.

Use the tool map's actual routes and allowances. Default charged usage to zero. Do not collect contacts, reveal emails, infer buying authority, or invent employee counts, revenue, installed technology, or buying intent.

## 4. Rank transparently

Propose a small understandable model, or use the participant's existing model. Show criteria, weights when requested, evidence, and unknowns. Keep eligibility separate from priority: no score overrides an exclusion or unresolved relationship hold.

Give each unique account a reason for and against inclusion and an evidence-confidence note. Do not replace missing evidence with a favorable score. If a numeric model cannot handle unknowns honestly, return a priority band and review queue instead.

## 5. Review and hand off

Reconcile every original row as Included, Review, Held, Excluded, or Duplicate. Show raw rows, unique companies, missing identities, and duplicate links separately. Resolve identity conflicts before describing the list as reviewed.

Return the ranked list, criteria, exceptions, and source register. Ask the participant to review the consequential rules and holds. Preserve stable account IDs for Campaign Segmentation and Account Brief Builder. Stop before enrichment, CRM changes, audience upload, or outreach.

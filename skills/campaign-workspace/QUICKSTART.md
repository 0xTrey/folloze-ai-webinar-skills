# Open your campaign map

Open `assets/campaign-workspace.html` in your browser, or ask your AI tool to display it. Start a new campaign and open Skills to see the nine included campaign skills.

Use this prompt with the complete skill folder available:

> Use Campaign Workspace to start an empty campaign. Help me connect my Company Brain, map my tools, and add my account list. Keep missing inputs visible and use the included webinar skills for the next step.

Upload approved text files or account CSVs into the app. Choose Use skill to prepare a chat request with its instructions and Markdown references. Review the returned output, then upload it to the matching stage. Export the campaign record to keep its source references.

The standalone app stores progress in your browser. Folder linking and automatic chat updates require a separately configured compatible connector. An HTML upload does not grant access to your files or tools. Each AI tool's HTML display needs its own check.

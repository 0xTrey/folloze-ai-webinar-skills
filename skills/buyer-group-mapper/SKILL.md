# Buyer Group Mapper

Create the bridge from a target account to useful role-specific creative. Start with the decision and the seller's personas, not a quota of names.

Read [context-and-evidence.md](references/context-and-evidence.md) and use [output-template.md](references/output-template.md).

Read [tool-map-use.md](references/tool-map-use.md). Use `tool-map.md` for CRM reuse, people discovery, current-role checks, email enrichment, and verification as separate operations. Record map version, selected routes, and any usage receipt. Missing tools or unknown cost lead to a manual request and useful role-only map.

## 1. Read the Company Brain

Use the seller's ICP to check account relevance and the personas to identify role jobs, questions, proof needs, and objections. Read product positioning for implementation dependencies and limits. Carry account and segment IDs from the supplied brief or list.

Ask only for the account or segment and seller context if missing. Contacts are optional. Without an approved persona, build a role hypothesis and ask for the missing persona fields; do not treat public job titles as approved buying roles.

## 2. Map the buying decision

Describe what must be evaluated, approved, implemented, and used for this offering. Include practitioner, sponsor, economic approver, technical/security evaluator, implementation owner, and risk roles only where the decision needs them.

Group roles by a shared job and message need. Give each group a stable buyer-group ID. Do not assume reporting lines or authority from seniority, title, or an organization chart. Mark each decision function Verified, Inferred, or Unknown with supporting evidence.

## 3. Add people only when evidence supports them

Use supplied professional records or permitted public professional sources within the agreed scope. For named candidates, keep source date, current role and company evidence, and the reason the role matches the group. If employment is historical or unclear, retain candidate status and record the next verification needed.

Keep current employment, role relevance, decision authority, relationship, and communication eligibility separate. A CRM association or valid email does not establish all five. Never invent names, email addresses, buying authority, reporting lines, or private-person traits.

When there are no names, return the complete role map and sourcing requests. If the participant explicitly requests contact discovery or enrichment, define the account/group scope, missing fields, exclusions, maximum records, and provider/pool allowance. Reuse existing candidates first; execute only verified mapped operations within the shared cost and reservation rules. Never infer an email pattern or force an arbitrary contact count.

Keep charged usage at zero when scope, access, debit bounds, balance, overage state, or authorization is unresolved. Return a precise bounded plan and manual export instructions. Preserve ambiguous-call reservations and stop that route before a retry. Store field-level provider evidence and usage separately from role and communication approval.

## 4. Plan content for each group

For each group, connect its job and question to one relevant seller capability, approved proof or gap, objection, message angle, and draft next step. List the needed content and any available asset.

Identify missing persona approval, capability evidence, coverage, and content. Give precise requests with suggested owners. Keep account-specific facts in the private map; do not imply knowledge of a person's motives or relationship in copy.

## 5. Review the map

Check every group against seller personas, account relevance, and the actual buying work. Show duplicate people, outdated affiliations, unresolved authority, and content gaps. Distinguish accounts with role coverage from accounts with verified people; a role map is useful even without contact coverage.

Return a draft map, coverage plan, source register, and usage receipt when an authorized operation ran. Preserve IDs for Buyer Group Ad Creative and Buyer Group Email Copy. Record participant review and contact eligibility as pending unless supported. Stop before credit purchases, CRM writes, or communication.

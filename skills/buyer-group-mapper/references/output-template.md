# Buyer-group map template

Return `buyer-groups.md`, optional `buyer-candidates.csv`, and `buyer-coverage.md`. Keep names in the participant's approved private workspace.

## buyer-groups.md

- Seller/product and Company Brain version
- Buying decision, account IDs, segment IDs, as-of date
- Persona sources and approval scope
- Tool-map ID/version, selected contact routes, limits/authorization state, and manual alternatives
- Review state and owner

| Buyer-group ID | Account/segment ID | Role family | Job and decision function | Authority status and evidence | Questions/objection | Proof need | Relevant capability/limit | Draft message angle | Content/CTA | Sources | Evidence/approval |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BG-1 | {IDs} | {roles} | {work and function} | {Verified, Inferred or Unknown; evidence} | {need} | {approved proof or gap} | {supported context} | {hypothesis} | {asset and next step} | {IDs/sections} | {labels} |

One group can span several accounts when their message needs match; retain explicit account membership. One person can participate in more than one decision function when supported, without becoming two contacts.

## buyer-candidates.csv or table

| Candidate ID | Account ID | Buyer-group IDs | Name/professional source | Current company/role evidence and date | Historical employer if relevant | Role relevance | Decision authority | Relationship | Communication eligibility | Next verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| {ID} | {account} | {groups} | {approved professional data or no candidate} | {verified, candidate, stale, unknown; locator/date} | {known or unknown} | {supported or hypothesis} | {verified, inferred, unknown} | {known or unknown} | {not assessed, held, or participant-confirmed scope} | {request} |

Email addresses are unnecessary for this planning output. Include them only when relevant, supplied or verified, authorized, and kept private. This skill does not establish deliverability or outreach permission.

For requested enrichment, record each returned field's provider, operation, source time, identity match, and verification result. Attach a private usage receipt with approval, pool, reserved upper bound, actual debit or held unknown debit, and balance readback. Do not promote catch-all, unknown, or expired results to verified email.

## buyer-coverage.md

- Accounts with role maps versus accounts with current verified professional candidates
- Missing role, persona approval, authority, contact, proof, or content
- Precise evidence request and suggested owner
- Existing assets mapped to questions and buying work
- Source register with locations, dates, allowed use, and limits
- Participant review, unresolved decisions, and next action
- Handoff IDs and group message briefs for ad and email skills

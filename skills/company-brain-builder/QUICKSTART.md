# Company Brain Builder

Give your AI the company context it needs to draft better marketing. Start with your company URL; add approved documents when you have them.

## Install or open

Unzip the download. Keep the `company-brain-builder` folder and its references together.

For Codex, copy that folder into your personal `.codex/skills` folder, or the `skills` folder under your configured Codex home. Open a new session and invoke `$company-brain-builder`. Follow your workspace's skill installation rules if it uses a different location.

For another approved AI tool, open `SKILL.md` and the referenced files in the same conversation or project. If the tool cannot load a folder, attach the Markdown files individually. This manual path works without a skill installer or a cloud connection.

## Start here

> Use Company Brain Builder for https://YOUR-COMPANY-DOMAIN. Start with public research. Walk me through five steps and help me find missing documents. Keep the brain in {Google Drive, OneDrive, or a local folder}. I will provide approved files as we go. Keep facts, draft ideas, and approval separate. Return files for manual saving if you cannot access my chosen location.

Use `$company-brain-builder` in place of the skill name when invoking it in Codex.

## Your five steps

1. Research your company from its website and official sources.
2. Collect your approved brand, ICP, messaging, positioning, and persona documents.
3. Get precise requests for missing documents and draft scaffolds when they do not exist.
4. Build five context files with sources, owners, dates, and clear draft labels.
5. Review the brain, test three marketing tasks, and load it in your approved AI workspace.

You do not need all five documents to begin. The AI can research and draft while you find them.

## What you get

A `company-brain` folder with Branding + Voice, Ideal customer profile, Messaging docs, Product positioning, and Buyer personas. Supporting files track sources, gaps, proof, and review.

You choose Google Drive, OneDrive, or a local folder. If the AI cannot read your storage, upload approved exports or paste approved excerpts. If it cannot save there, it returns files for you to upload or save.

Public research is a starting point. Your authorized owners approve internal strategy and usable claims. The AI gives you document requests to copy; it does not message your colleagues.

After review, attach the relevant brain files to a marketing task. Ask the AI to cite its context and show missing or conflicting information. Pick one owner to keep the files current.

## Bring your tool map

Attach `tool-map.md` from Tools & Data Setup with your company context. The skill checks the route in this workspace and records its map version. Without a map, it can use approved files or build a small provisional map; charged usage stays at zero until specifically authorized. Owned tools without a connection use manual export/upload instructions.

# Find the missing documents

Give the participant only the requests that fill actual gaps. These are suggested roles; ask the participant to confirm who owns each area.

| Area | Ask for | Search terms | Suggested owner | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Branding + Voice | Current brand guide, writing guide, approved copy examples, and official asset folder | brand guidelines, tone of voice, style guide, brand assets | Brand or creative lead; content lead for voice | Prevents wrong tone, invented design rules, and unapproved assets |
| Ideal customer profile | Current ICP with qualification criteria, exclusions, priority segments, and review date | ICP, ideal customer, target segments, qualification, exclusions | Product marketing or revenue operations | Separates plausible audiences from approved targeting |
| Messaging docs | Current message house, audience messages, objections, approved proof, and language restrictions | messaging framework, message house, value proposition, objections | Product marketing or content lead | Gives campaigns specific, consistent language |
| Product positioning | Current positioning brief, product overview, competitive context, capabilities, and limits | positioning, product brief, differentiation, product overview | Product marketing; product manager for capabilities | Prevents overstated features, comparisons, and roadmap promises |
| Buyer personas | Current role-based personas with jobs, pains, buying involvement, objections, and proof needs | buyer persona, buying committee, customer research, jobs to be done | Product marketing or customer insights | Connects the message to the role's decision |

Search filenames first within the folder the participant authorizes. Check the content, version, owner, and approval record of relevant matches. Avoid raw CRM records and interview transcripts; ask for approved summaries.

## Copyable request

> Please point me to the current approved {document} for {company/product}. I need {missing fields} for {marketing task}. Include its owner, review date, and whether we may use it in {approved AI workspace}. An approved summary is enough if the full file is restricted.

Return this text to the participant. Do not send it to anyone.

## When no document exists

Offer a short draft in the corresponding core file. Keep source-backed observations separate from inferred recommendations and unanswered fields. Ask only the question needed for the next useful decision.

| Area | Draft scaffold | First review question |
|---|---|---|
| Branding + Voice | We sound {traits}; use {example}; avoid {terms}; assets {location or missing}; visual rules {supplied or missing} | Which approved example sounds most like your company? |
| Ideal customer profile | Best fit {traits}; problem {job}; exclude {criteria or missing}; qualify with {questions}; thresholds {known or missing} | What makes an account a poor fit? |
| Messaging docs | For {audience}, solve {problem} with {value}; support with {proof or missing}; next step {action}; avoid {claims} | Which value claim can we support today? |
| Product positioning | For {audience}, {product} is a {category} that {job}; alternatives {known or missing}; difference {supported or draft}; limits {known or missing} | What can this product do today, and what is out of scope? |
| Buyer personas | Role {title}; job {task}; pain {problem}; influence {known or missing}; questions {topics}; proof {needed}; message {draft} | What does this role need to believe before moving forward? |

Public research may suggest the filled values. Internal approval remains pending until an authorized owner confirms the specific content and use.

# Tools & Data Setup

Map the tools you already use so the other skills can choose available data sources and honest manual alternatives.

## Install or open

Unzip `tools-data-setup` and keep its references together. Copy it into your Codex skills folder and open a new session, following your workspace's installation rules. In another approved AI tool, attach `SKILL.md` and its three references in one conversation or project.

## Start here

> Use $tools-data-setup. I use {tool names I know} for documents, CRM, contacts/enrichment, engagement/intent, and creative. Check what is actually available in this AI workspace. Create a tool map with verified access, data scope, operation costs, credit limits, and manual alternatives. Use zero charged operations during setup. Keep the map in {Google Drive, OneDrive, or a local folder}, or return files for manual saving.

You can name only the tools you know. The skill asks for missing essentials and keeps unknown capabilities unverified. It will not ask for passwords or require a new service.

## What you get

`tool-map.md` records each useful operation and how to use it. `usage-ledger.csv` starts a shared record for later authorized credit usage. Ownership, connection, tested access, and permission remain separate.

Contact search, employment checks, email reveal, and verification each have their own route and costs. Later enrichment needs an explicit account/field scope and allowance; available subscription credits alone do not authorize spending.

Attach the tool map with your Company Brain and relevant account outputs when running another skill. If the AI cannot access a tool, it gives you a precise export or upload request. Review access after changes and refresh balances before charged work.

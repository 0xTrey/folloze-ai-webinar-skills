# Account brief template

Return `account-brief-{account-id}.md` and a source register, which may be a section in the brief.

```markdown
# {Target company}: account brief

- Account ID and domain: {stable ID and verified or unresolved identity}
- Segment ID: {provided ID or unassigned}
- Seller/product: {seller scope}
- Company Brain version: {version or supplied-summary reference}
- Tool map and routes: {map ID/version, route IDs, access/coverage, manual alternatives}
- As of: {date and timezone}
- Goal and draft CTA: {objective and action}
- Review state and owner: {Draft; owner or suggested role}
- Recommended next action: {one bounded action and dependency}

## What does this company do?

{Products, customers, relevant operating context, source IDs, dates, and unknowns.}

## What do we already know?

{Relationship, customer/opportunity status, historical needs, owner, overlap, and source coverage.}

## Have they engaged with us?

{Observed event types, original records, period, coverage, and unobserved sources.}

## Are they researching our category?

{Exact intent topic/window and source, or Missing. No seller-interest claim from a category score.}

## What is their relevant operating setup?

{Observed workflow or technology evidence, its limits, and missing prerequisites.}

## What is changing in their business?

{Dated primary announcement or supplied source; relevance stated as a hypothesis.}

## Why might our offering matter?

{One account-specific premise, ICP findings, supported seller capability, role hypothesis, proof or gap.}

## Campaign decision

- Test: {smallest useful campaign hypothesis}
- Intended buyer group: {persona-based draft or supplied group ID}
- Learning question and proposed measure: {no invented baseline}
- Owner: {confirmed or suggested}
- Holds and open questions: {identity, fit, relationship, proof, or access}
- Next action: {review or evidence request}

## Source register

| ID | Claim/event | Original URL or record/section | Source date and reporting period | Access date | Coverage/limits | Evidence | Approval/use scope |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SRC-1 | {fact} | {locator} | {date/window or unknown} | {date} | {complete, partial, unavailable, conflict} | {label} | {state and authority or unknown} |

## Review and handoff

{Corrections, unresolved decisions, authority, account/segment IDs, buyer-map input, and file save state.}
```

Use a meaningful question even when its answer is Missing. For non-marketing buyers, describe the relevant operating setup rather than forcing a martech section. Keep confidential history in the private brief and exclude it from external copy.

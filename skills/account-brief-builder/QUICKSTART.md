# Account Brief Builder

An account brief covering company context, relationship, engagement, category research, operating setup, business changes, and seller relevance.

## Install or open

Unzip the individual download and keep the `account-brief-builder` folder intact. For Codex, copy it into your personal `.codex/skills` folder or the `skills` folder under your configured Codex home, then open a new session. Follow your workspace's installation rules if it uses another location.

In another approved AI tool, attach `SKILL.md` and all reference files in the same conversation or project. No integration is required for the manual path.

## Bring this

A target company URL and seller context. Add a segment, goal, or approved account notes when available. Uploaded documents and approved excerpts work when your storage connection is unavailable.

## Start here

> Use $account-brief-builder for {target company and URL}. Our seller context is in the attached Company Brain. Use the attached segment and account IDs. Research primary sources, add only authorized account context, and return a cited brief with one campaign hypothesis and visible gaps.

In tools without skill invocation, replace the `$` name with the skill's title and attach its files.

## Use the result

Review the actual output and its sources. Facts, inferred guidance, unknowns, and approval stay separate. Keep the same account, segment, and buyer-group IDs when moving to the next task.

Choose Google Drive, OneDrive, or a local folder for the returned files. If the AI cannot save there, upload or save them manually. Use private company material only in your approved AI workspace.

## Bring your tool map

Attach `tool-map.md` from Tools & Data Setup with your company context. The skill checks the route in this workspace and records its map version. Without a map, it can use approved files or build a small provisional map; charged usage stays at zero until specifically authorized. Owned tools without a connection use manual export/upload instructions.

# Buyer Group Email Copy

One writing skill for AgentMail, Smartlead or another sender. It turns company context, approved pitches and buyer roles into reviewable email drafts and platform handoffs.

## Install or open

Unzip the individual download and keep the `buyer-group-email-copy` folder intact. For Codex, copy it into your personal `.codex/skills` folder or the `skills` folder under your configured Codex home, then open a new session. Follow your workspace's installation rules if it uses another location.

In another approved AI tool, attach `SKILL.md` and all reference files in the same conversation or project. No integration is required for the manual path.

## Bring this

Company Brain or seller summary, approved pitch library, account brief, buyer roles, useful content and campaign destination. Named recipients are optional. Uploaded files work without a connected storage tool.

## Start here

> Use $buyer-group-email-copy with my Company Brain, account brief, approved pitches and content catalog. Draft five role-specific emails for this account using value subjects, problem or customer-story openings and content CTAs. Prepare an AgentMail handoff for review. Do not send.

For Smartlead, replace “AgentMail handoff” with “Smartlead CSV and sequence template.” Supply your own approved pitches, content and campaign board URL.

In tools without skill invocation, replace the `$` name with the skill's title and attach its files.

## Use the result

Review the actual output and its sources. The five sections are value subject, problem/story opening, company-and-role bridge, fixed approved pitch and useful content CTA. Keep the same account, segment and buyer-group IDs. The offline checker catches structural gaps and exact same-company repeats across supplied platform history; a reviewer checks claims and near duplicates.

The skill installs in Codex or another supported AI workspace. It does not install inside AgentMail or Smartlead, create a connection, import contacts or activate sending. Use the approved sending workflow after reviewing the draft packet.

Choose Google Drive, OneDrive, or a local folder for the returned files. If the AI cannot save there, upload or save them manually. Use private company material only in your approved AI workspace.

## Bring your tool map

Attach `tool-map.md` from Tools & Data Setup with your company context. The skill checks the route in this workspace and records its map version. Without a map, it can use approved files or build a small provisional map; charged usage stays at zero until specifically authorized. Owned tools without a connection use manual export/upload instructions.

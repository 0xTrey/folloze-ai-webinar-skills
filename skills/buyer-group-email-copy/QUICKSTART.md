# Buyer Group Email Copy

Short email drafts by group, source notes, link state, and review gaps. No email is sent or enrolled.

## Install or open

Unzip the individual download and keep the `buyer-group-email-copy` folder intact. For Codex, copy it into your personal `.codex/skills` folder or the `skills` folder under your configured Codex home, then open a new session. Follow your workspace's installation rules if it uses another location.

In another approved AI tool, attach `SKILL.md` and all reference files in the same conversation or project. No integration is required for the manual path.

## Bring this

Seller voice/product context, a buyer group, and one desired action. Named recipients are optional. Uploaded documents and approved excerpts work when your storage connection is unavailable.

## Start here

> Use $buyer-group-email-copy with my Company Brain, account briefs, and buyer-group map. Draft one concise unsent email per group with up to two subjects and one ask. Keep personalization evidence outside the copy and omit held claims.

In tools without skill invocation, replace the `$` name with the skill's title and attach its files.

## Use the result

Review the actual output and its sources. Facts, inferred guidance, unknowns, and approval stay separate. Keep the same account, segment, and buyer-group IDs when moving to the next task.

Choose Google Drive, OneDrive, or a local folder for the returned files. If the AI cannot save there, upload or save them manually. Use private company material only in your approved AI workspace.

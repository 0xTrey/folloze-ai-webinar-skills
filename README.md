# Folloze AI Webinar Skills

Build campaign context, map your audience, and create reviewable drafts with eight reusable skills from the Folloze AI webinar.

**[Download all eight skills](downloads/folloze-ai-webinar-skills.zip?raw=1)** · [Start here](START-HERE.md)

| Skill | What it helps you do | Download |
|---|---|---|
| [Campaign Workspace](skills/campaign-workspace/SKILL.md) | Open the Folloze campaign map and track sources, gaps, and review | [ZIP](downloads/campaign-workspace.zip?raw=1) |
| [Tools & Data Setup](skills/tools-data-setup/SKILL.md) | Map your tools, access, credit limits, and manual alternatives | [ZIP](downloads/tools-data-setup.zip?raw=1) |
| [Company Brain Builder](skills/company-brain-builder/SKILL.md) | Assemble brand, ICP, messaging, positioning, and persona context | [ZIP](downloads/company-brain-builder.zip?raw=1) |
| [Campaign Segmentation](skills/campaign-segmentation/SKILL.md) | Turn your account list into explainable groups | [ZIP](downloads/campaign-segmentation.zip?raw=1) |
| [Account Brief Builder](skills/account-brief-builder/SKILL.md) | Connect account evidence to campaign hypotheses | [ZIP](downloads/account-brief-builder.zip?raw=1) |
| [Buyer Group Mapper](skills/buyer-group-mapper/SKILL.md) | Map roles, known contacts, and coverage gaps | [ZIP](downloads/buyer-group-mapper.zip?raw=1) |
| [Buyer Group Ad Creative](skills/buyer-group-ad-creative/SKILL.md) | Draft group-specific ads and visual briefs | [ZIP](downloads/buyer-group-ad-creative.zip?raw=1) |

Start with your company URL and the tools you already use. Add approved documents and an account list when available. Each skill includes instructions, a quick start, and its required references. Campaign Workspace also includes a standalone HTML app.

Use your AI tool's supported skill-installation flow. For a file-based chat, attach the skill instructions and referenced files together. Your documents, contacts, connections, and credentials belong in your own approved workspace.

Marketo email building, sales email building, sales cadences, and other campaign skills can be added later. The current email skill prepares copy; it does not send or enroll contacts. New creative and strategy need your review.

Download hashes and file inventories are in [the manifest](downloads/manifest.json). Maintainers can rebuild the ZIPs with `python3 build-pack.py`. The build verifies the file allowlist, portable paths, references, and archive bytes.
